FAQ

Questions? Good.

Do the bottles need refrigeration?

Not until you open them. Sealed bottles live at room temperature and take zero fridge space. Once opened, keep refrigerated — it’s a fully natural product.

How many drinks come out of one bottle?

One bottle of essence makes 20–25 glasses. At recommended menu prices that’s the margin story your accountant will love.

What’s actually inside?

Premium tea leaves and edible botanicals — nothing else. No preservatives, no added flavors or fragrances, no artificial sweeteners.

How hard is it for my staff?

Four steps: glass with water and ice, add essence with our measuring tool, stir, serve. Any barista learns it in a minute.

Do you have sugar-free options?

Yes — Detox and Fresh come in sugar-free versions, same price, same flavor logic.
